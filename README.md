## 🔍 Key Analysis

### 🎓 Attrition by Education
Analyzes employee attrition across different educational backgrounds and identifies education groups with higher attrition.

### 👨‍👩‍👧 Attrition by Gender
Compares employee attrition between male and female employees.

### 💼 Attrition by Job Role
Identifies job roles experiencing higher employee attrition, including:
- Laboratory Technician
- Sales Executive
- Research Scientist
- Sales Representative
- Human Resources
- Manufacturing Director
- Healthcare Representative
- Manager
- Research Director

### 💰 Attrition by Salary Slab
Analyzes attrition across different salary ranges to understand the relationship between compensation and employee turnover.

### 📅 Attrition by Work Experience
Visualizes employee attrition based on years of experience in the company.

### 🏢 Department Analysis
Interactive department filters allow users to explore HR and attrition metrics across different departments.

## 🎯 Objective

The objective of this project is to transform raw employee data into an **interactive HR analytics dashboard** that helps identify employee attrition patterns and provides insights into workforce-related factors such as salary, job role, education, gender, age, department, and work experience.

## 💡 Business Questions Answered

This dashboard helps answer questions such as:

- What is the overall employee attrition rate?
- How many employees have left the organization?
- Which job roles have the highest attrition?
- How does attrition vary across salary slabs?
- Which education groups show higher attrition?
- How does attrition differ by gender?
- How does employee experience relate to attrition?
- Which departments can be further investigated for employee turnover?
